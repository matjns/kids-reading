Reading Quest for Mac

This copy is not from the Mac App Store. macOS may say it cannot be opened
because Apple cannot check it for malicious software.

To open it:

1. Unzip the download if it is still zipped.
2. Move "Reading Quest.app" to your Applications folder.
3. Control-click (or right-click) the app and choose Open.
4. Choose Open again.

If macOS still blocks it:

- Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
- Click Open Anyway.

Or, in Terminal:

xattr -cr "/Applications/Reading Quest.app"

Then open the app again.

Reading Quest does not use the internet. Stars and an optional name stay on
this Mac. The name starts blank.

Closing the window or pressing Command-Q quits the app.
